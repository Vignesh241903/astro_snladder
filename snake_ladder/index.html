Help · Tutorial

Goal: be the first commander to land exactly on tile 100.

  1. Turns: press ROLL DICE to move that many tiles. Turns alternate, unless a Magic Hole grants a bonus roll.
  2. Overshoot: a roll that would pass 100 holds your position until a roll lands exactly on it.
  3. ▲ Space Ladders climb you forward.
  4. ★ Cosmic Shooting Stars carry you back down their trail.
  5. ● Black Holes pull you far back. The dark core swallows your token and spits it out lower.
  6. ◎ Wormholes teleport you to their exit.
  7. ✦ Magic Holes grant a bonus roll.
  8. Settings: press S or SETTINGS for music, sound, bot pace and accessibility.
